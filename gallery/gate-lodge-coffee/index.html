The Space

Small by design, not by accident.

Gate Lodge started as a shipping container on Model Farm Road — and stayed one, on purpose. No knockthrough, no extension, just a tight, spotless space fitted out to do a handful of things properly: good coffee, a short brunch menu, and service that doesn't feel rushed.

Minimal, on purpose. Fewer seats, fewer distractions, more attention on what's in the cup and on the plate. Walk in any day of the week — there's no queue system and no booking, just a window and a welcome.
