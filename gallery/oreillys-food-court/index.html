The Stall

One counter. A few things, done right.

O'Reilly's is one stall, not a strip of them — a single counter turning out burgers off the flat-top, wraps built to order, and pizza straight from the oven. No sprawling menu, no trying to be everything: just a short list of things worth queuing for.

Lively on purpose. This isn't a quiet sit-down room — it's Cork on a Friday night, plates moving, the grill going, and a menu tight enough that everything on it is good.
