Our Story

Built on a forecourt, not a shopfront.

Thrive Coffee started life as a petrol station in Bishopstown — the kind of spot you'd drive past without a second look. Instead of knocking it down, we reformed it: the pumps came out, the window went in, and it became one of the simplest ways in Cork to get a genuinely good coffee without leaving your car.

New age, simple. That's the whole idea. Fresh coffee, friendly staff, and a drive-thru that doesn't feel like a compromise. If you'd rather stretch your legs, you're welcome to walk in and skip the line entirely.
