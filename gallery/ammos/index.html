How it works

Order, buzz, collect

No app, no counter queue — just order, wait for the buzz, and grab your food hot.

1

Order at the truck

Walk up, have a look at what's on, and place your order with whoever's on the grill.

2

Take a buzzer

You'll be handed a buzzer. Grab a seat at the outside tables while we get cooking.

3

Buzz, then collect

When it goes off, that's your food ready — come back up and collect it hot.
