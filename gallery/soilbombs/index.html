Soil Bombs is a Transition Year mini-company from Mount Mercy College, Cork — a team of young entrepreneurs turning a sustainable idea into a real, growing business.

Our story

Made by students, rooted in sustainability

What started as a Student Enterprise Programme project grew into something we're seriously proud of. We wanted to make plant care simple, satisfying and kind to the earth — so we packed everything tired soil needs into one little bomb.

From local markets to the SEP National Finals, we've been on an incredible journey — and we're only just getting started.
