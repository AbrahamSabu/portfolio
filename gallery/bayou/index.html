Cork City · New Orleans Soul

A little corner of Louisiana, right in the middle of Cork.

Bayou started with a simple obsession: the food of Louisiana, cooked the way it demands to be cooked — a dark roux built over a full hour, gumbo that takes its time, beignets fried to order. We brought it to a bright room on Oliver Plunkett Street, plated it on blue enamel, and opened the doors for breakfast. Come hungry.

The experience

The Kitchen

One hour. Every time.

A roux isn't rushed. Ours is cooked low for a full hour until it's the colour of dark chocolate — the foundation of the gumbo and everything around it.

Cooked slow, served all day.

The Room

Bright, easy, all yours.

Pressed-tin ceilings, big windows, marble tables and a wall of plants. A sunny corner of Cork that happens to taste like Louisiana.

Walk in. Stay a while.

The Counter

Coffee & cold bottles.

A proper café au lait the New Orleans way, filter coffee that keeps coming, and a fridge full of colourful craft sodas. No bar — just good stuff.

Beignets pair with everything.
