Privacy & cookies

Short version: we collect what we need to drive you somewhere, and we measure nothing until you say yes.

What we collect

When you send a booking request we receive your name, phone number, email address and the details of the journey. We use those to quote the fare, run the job, and contact you about it. That is the whole purpose.

What we do not do

We do not sell your details, add you to a marketing list, or pass them to third parties for their own purposes. Confirm retention period is how long we keep booking records before deleting them.

Cookies and analytics

This site sets no analytics or marketing cookies until you accept them in the banner. If you decline, nothing is loaded and the site works exactly the same. Fonts are served from this domain rather than a third party, so visiting the site does not disclose your IP address to anyone else.

Your rights

Under GDPR you can ask what we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Email info@jetexecutivetravel.ie and we will deal with it. If you are unhappy with how we handle that, you can complain to the Irish Data Protection Commission at dataprotection.ie.
