Our Story

Born in Cork, 2003. Named for the music.

Café Depeche has been part of Cork life since 2003 — a Lancaster Quay institution with a fireplace in the corner, walls lined with framed music press, and a Depeche Mode mural that gives the place its name. It's coffee for the masses, served with a bit of soul.

The brunch is proper and generous, the latte art is taken seriously, and the welcome is pure Cork. We were proud to be named Oceanic Regional Winner at the Food Awards Ireland 2024 — and we still cook every plate like we're trying to win it again.
